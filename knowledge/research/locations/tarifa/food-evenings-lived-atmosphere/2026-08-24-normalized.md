## Core synthesis

Tarifa belongs to the regional spring almadraba tradition, while Baelo Claudia provides an ancient fish-processing context. The old town’s plazas and narrow streets support a walkable evening circuit among mixed uses.

## Publication boundary

Menu provenance, events and venues are volatile. The evidence does not establish a universal quiet, lively, bohemian, family or party atmosphere; that requires dated operator fieldwork by micro-location and season.
