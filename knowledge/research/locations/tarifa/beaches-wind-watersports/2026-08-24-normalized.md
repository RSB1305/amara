## Core synthesis

Tarifa’s beaches form a two-sided system: Playa Chica beside the island causeway and the Atlantic sequence from Los Lances toward Valdevaqueros, Punta Paloma and Bolonia. A developed tuition market is verified, while suitability must be decided from live wind, sea, flags, zones, crowding and instruction.

## Publication boundary

No beach is universally sheltered, family-safe or beginner-suitable. Lifeguards, facilities, launch zones, conservation measures, parking and providers are live-check data.
