## Core synthesis

The Strait concentrates intercontinental bird migration within a protected maritime-terrestrial landscape. Long-term Migres monitoring, land viewpoints, coastal trails and authorised cetacean excursions make the movement legible to visitors.

## Publication boundary

No species sighting is guaranteed. A seasonal likelihood calendar needs multi-year data. Excursion authorisation, trail conditions and viewpoint access require current verification; cetacean viewing is governed by national protection rules.
