## Core synthesis

Tangier is Tarifa’s most distinctive outer-ring excursion because a direct passenger ferry reaches Tangier Ville. Gibraltar adds a border-and-Upper-Rock day; Vejer offers an elevated white-town contrast; Cádiz offers a larger maritime historic city.

## Publication boundary

Ferry operation, border rules, passport/visa requirements, check-in, insurance, access and total journey time are high-volatility. The indicative one-hour sea crossing is not total excursion time. Tangier remains an outer-ring experience, not a seventh core Tarifa spoke.
