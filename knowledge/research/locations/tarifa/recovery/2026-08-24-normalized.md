# Tarifa existing corpus recovery — normalized

## Result

The pre-existing corpus was preserved rather than replaced. It covers geography/orientation, stay areas, climate, winter, parking/accessibility, shopping, health and practical rules. Existing checked-in Tarifa facts and open questions remain authoritative for those topics.

## Recovered artifacts

- Ten GPT/Gemini raw reports across eight topics.
- One operator-provided Tarifa welcome guide.
- Existing repository baseline: four fact modules, seven practical open-question modules, eight page manifests, one source module and one research synthesis.

## New coverage decision

The recovery exposed six missing destination layers: identity/history, beaches/wind, Strait nature, Bolonia/Baelo, food/evenings and day trips. These were researched as separate runs and normalized without creating pages, routes or public copy.

## Governing boundaries

- Preserve “Europe’s southernmost mainland city”; reject unqualified “southernmost point of Europe”.
- Never infer property-level wind, views, distances or atmosphere.
- Treat schedules, access, prices, operators, regulations and weather as volatile.
- Location owns stay selection; Experience owns what guests can do.
