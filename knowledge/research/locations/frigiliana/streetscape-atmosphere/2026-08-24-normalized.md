# Frigiliana streetscape and overnight atmosphere — normalized dossier

## Evidence result

The official heritage record supports a precise visual-spatial explanation beyond “charming white village”: a compact white exterior mass; contour-following streets; steep stepped transverse links; different Barribarto and Barribajo parcel/building patterns; adarves and algorfas; fragmented, interlocking buildings; and landmark scale changes at El Ingenio, San Antonio and the castle hill.

The protected-ensemble maintenance rules require white exterior coatings and preservation of each original wooden exterior element’s design, colour and material. No universal blue or turquoise door rule was found.

## First-hand result

The operator experiences the doors as individually varied in colour and the village as especially quiet and atmospheric in the evening. These observations are stored as first-party evidence rather than being laundered through generic travel sources.

They are not universal guarantees. Door inventories change; evening conditions vary by route, season, weekday, festival, hospitality activity and accommodation position.

## Next evidence

The highest-value next step is a lightweight repeated field protocol: one defined Barribarto–Barribajo route, dated non-identifying façade photographs, and day/evening observations across shoulder season, summer and a festival period. This can strengthen overnight-oriented authoring without invading resident privacy.
