# Frigiliana parking — preserved baseline

## Purpose

This dossier preserves the parking knowledge already present in AMARA before the Evidence & Knowledge architecture was introduced. It is an evidence inventory, not public page copy and not a claim that every existing sentence is independently verified.

The primary input is `src/content/frigilianaParkingContent.ts`, first established in commit `851865e0` on 27 July 2026 and amended with a dated tariff observation in commit `1299fdfb` on 19 August 2026.

## Reusable sources registered

- `frigiliana-tourism-official-street-map`: official tourism orientation map; useful for village structure, not live parking terms.
- `frigiliana-plaza-garage-signage-2026-08`: first-party observation of the displayed tariff; currently lacks a stored photograph.
- `google-maps-frigiliana-live-parking-search`: discovery and orientation only, never authority for parking legality or terms.
- `amara-frigiliana-parking-editorial-baseline-2026-07`: preserves prior AMARA wording but is not independent evidence.

## Current evidence result

The pool can presently support a cautious planning narrative:

- Plaza de las Tres Culturas is a useful lower-village orientation point.
- A paid central car park was observed below the plaza in August 2026.
- The observed tariff was €0.0417 per minute with a €15 daily cap; this is highly volatile and must always carry its date.
- Historic-centre vehicle access should not be assumed, but the exact legal access regime is not yet documented in the pool.
- A final walk is often part of the parking plan; its gradient, surface and steps depend on the exact destination.
- Live signs govern the decision on legality, restrictions, access and tariffs.

The structured records in `knowledge/facts/locations/frigiliana/parking.ts` are the current reusable fact inventory. Their status and claim boundary take precedence over any shorthand in this dossier.

## Important gaps

The current pool does not yet establish:

- the garage owner or operator;
- live opening hours, height limit or payment methods;
- a current official tariff publication;
- the exact historic-centre vehicle restriction zone and exemptions;
- verified accessible bays, lifts or step-free routes;
- named legal/free outer-village street-parking zones;
- a generally legal visitor drop-off point.

These are recorded separately in `knowledge/open-questions/locations/frigiliana/parking.ts` so that a future Deep Research begins with the actual gaps instead of repeating the whole topic.

## Next research brief

Use municipal, concession/operator and dated on-site sources first. For every named parking facility or street area, capture location, legal status, tariff, hours, height limit, payment, accessibility, volatility and checked date. Preserve conflicts and outdated terms through explicit supersession; do not overwrite the old observation silently.

Do not copy long third-party documents into the repository unless reuse rights permit it. Store the source metadata, direct link, relevant short extract or observation, access date and exact claim supported.
