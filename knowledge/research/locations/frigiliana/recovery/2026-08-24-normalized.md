# Frigiliana semantic corpus recovery — normalized dossier

## Recovery result

The prior A–F research corpus contained substantial identity, history, agriculture, culture, landscape and seasonal knowledge that had not reached the canonical Frigiliana fact layer. Before this run, the destination fact folder covered geography, parking, where to stay and winter stays but did not preserve the main explanations for why Frigiliana looks, feels and functions as it does.

The recovered causal structure is:

- hillside topography and the Muslim/Morisco urban tradition shaped the protected street fabric;
- terracing, acequias and albercas made steep-slope cultivation possible;
- conquest, imposed Christianization, revolt, violence, expulsion and depopulation created a major discontinuity;
- repopulation and sugar production under new ownership produced El Ingenio, the Maquinillas and later expansion;
- modern public history uses the Three Cultures frame, but evidence for a permanent local Jewish community remains incomplete;
- the operator’s observations of door-colour variety, evening quiet and visible mango/avocado cultivation supply first-hand differentiation without pretending to be universal statistics.

Unresolved evidence needs are preserved separately under matching open-question files. The strongest conflict corrections are: San Antonio is Baroque rather than generically Renaissance; the Three Cultures festival is not proof of three-faith local coexistence; Maymón Leví’s property connection is not proof of residence or a community; and no official universal blue-door rule was found.

## Coverage decision

No new public page, slug or `src/content` change is authorized by this recovery. The knowledge now supports a later human-reviewed audit of existing Frigiliana destination content around five questions:

1. Why does the historic village have this form?
2. Which historical ruptures remain legible in it?
3. What does “Three Cultures” safely mean?
4. How did water and agriculture shape the visible landscape?
5. What changes when a guest stays into the evening?

The recovered research is larger than any single page. Page selection must preserve the unused facts in `knowledge/` rather than deleting them.
