# Frigiliana three cultures evidence — normalized dossier

## Evidence result

Frigiliana’s Muslim/Morisco and post-conquest Christian history is strongly supported by the Junta de Andalucía heritage decree. That record supports the urban imprint, imposed Christianization, the 1500 Mudéjar revolt, the 1568–1570 Morisco rupture, destruction of the castle, complete depopulation, later repopulation and municipal consolidation.

The Jewish evidence is narrower. Academic research supports a property relationship between Maymón Leví and the historic Taha of Frigiliana. It does not currently establish residence in the village, a synagogue, a resident Jewish community or harmonious simultaneous three-faith coexistence.

## Public-history boundary

The Festival Frigiliana 3 Culturas is documented from 2006 and is verified as a modern programme of culture, gastronomy, music and theatre. It is therefore reusable as evidence of present-day identity work. It must not be used as retroactive demographic proof.

Safe future authoring can connect three levels while naming them:

- material Muslim/Morisco heritage;
- conquest, violence, expulsion and repopulation;
- modern Three Cultures public history with a bounded Jewish evidence trail.

## Next evidence

Priority work is an archival check for local Jewish households or institutions, precise location of Maymón Leví’s property connection and a source audit of the ceramic historical panels. Until that work is complete, the open questions are intentional safeguards rather than content gaps to fill with marketing copy.
