# Frigiliana agriculture as lived landscape — normalized dossier

## Evidence result

The BIC record supports a continuous landscape explanation rather than a list of products: steep slopes were terraced; acequias and albercas distributed and stored water; garden crops and sugar cane were tied to the Muslim-period landscape; and later sugar processing used hydraulic power at El Ingenio and the Maquinillas.

For 2023, SIMA reports 746 hectares of woody crops in the municipality. Avocado was the leading irrigated woody crop at 250 hectares and oil olive the leading rain-fed woody crop at 270 hectares. The checked SIMA summary does not provide a municipal mango area.

Acciona’s Monte Ariza project page supplies a bounded provider claim—400 farmers and 231 hectares of avocado/mango crops using localized irrigation—but it is not an independent municipal crop or water audit. The operator’s observation that mango and avocado cultivation is visible remains a separate first-party fact.

## Guest-visibility boundary

Historic significance does not prove present public access. Before guest-facing advice, AMARA still needs a current inventory of visible and functional water infrastructure, the access status of El Ingenio and the Maquinillas, current cane/product provenance and traceable guest-facing producers or markets.

Current drought, water restrictions, crop seasons, opening arrangements and production status are volatile. They must not be inferred from the durable history.
