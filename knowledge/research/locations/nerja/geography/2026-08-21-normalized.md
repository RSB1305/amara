# Nerja geography & orientation — normalized dossier

## Evidence result

The verified geographic model is the relationship between a coast-facing principal settlement and a steep mountain hinterland:

- Nerja municipality covers 85.07 km²;
- SIMA gives the principal settlement a 22 m reference altitude, which must not be treated as a town-wide elevation;
- the municipality has four statistical population centres: Nerja, Maro, Imaroga and Macaca;
- Maro is therefore a distinct statistical centre within the same municipality, not merely a Nerja street label;
- the Sierra de Almijara rises close to the coast and includes numerous summits above 1,500 m;
- the official regional plan gives Navachica an elevation of 1,832 m;
- the lower Río Chíllar runs inland from its mouth toward the quarry, with a municipal maintenance section described as approximately 3.6 km.

This supports a page that explains municipal scale, coast, distinct settlement nuclei, river corridor and mountain backdrop without reducing Nerja to beaches or a single altitude.

## Conflicts and claim boundaries

The current evidence does not directly prove that Navachica is the highest point inside the current Nerja municipal polygon. That superlative remains an open GIS question.

Official tourism material in the raw research gives both 13 km and 14 km for the coastline. The conflict is stored as a disputed fact; neither figure should be used as exact canonical copy until the endpoints and measurement method are known.

The Río Chíllar’s location is stable geography. Access, closure, safety and environmental restrictions are volatile and require a current operational source. A maintenance article is not permission to enter.
