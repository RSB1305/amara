# Nerja coast, beaches and lived atmosphere — normalized dossier

Nerja’s coast is best understood as distinct beach pockets, access points and neighbourhood experiences around a cliff-shaped shore. El Salón and Calahonda sit beside the Balcón; this spatial relationship does not establish identical accessibility or a continuously flat coastal route.

AMARA Playa’s central-flat Torrecilla micro-location is operator-confirmed. Zone-specific evening-return quality is relevant to a stay, but exact walking times, gradients, steps and accessible alternatives remain open until measured.

Dated municipal operational evidence stays dated: the 2025 public-address list does not guarantee current services, and the 2022 coastal-path update does not establish 2026 continuity. Repeat-guest feedback that Nerja felt too busy is retained only as bounded internal evidence.
