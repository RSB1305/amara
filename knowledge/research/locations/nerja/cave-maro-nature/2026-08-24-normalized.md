# Nerja cave, Maro and nature — normalized dossier

The Cueva de Nerja is a protected archaeological zone and karst-hydrological system beside Maro, not merely a show cave. Its modern discovery on 12 January 1959, public opening on 12 June 1960 and 2006 BIC designation are verified. The public route must not be equated with the full protected system, and disputed or evolving art-dating claims require study-level evidence.

Maro-Cerro Gordo covers about 1,913 hectares across Nerja and Almuñécar, mostly marine. Cliffs, coves, beaches, cultivated terraces and Posidonia habitat form one maritime-terrestrial landscape with overlapping conservation designations.

Schedules, tickets, vehicle rules, shuttles and route status remain live utility data. Conflicting official cliff-height figures are intentionally not resolved by guesswork.
