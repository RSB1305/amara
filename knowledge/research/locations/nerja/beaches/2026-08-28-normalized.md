# Nerja and Frigiliana beaches and connections — normalized dossier

## Publication boundaries

- Never promise quiet, easy parking, barrier-free access, calm water, space or open services.
- Dated 2026 shuttle times, prices, Blue Flags and mobility equipment are high-volatility facts.
- Cantarriján is not in Nerja municipality; proximity does not transfer geographic ownership.
- `Playazo-Chucho` is a certification scope, not permission to collapse two beach names.
- External research cannot become an AMARA first-hand recommendation.
